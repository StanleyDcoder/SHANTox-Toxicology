A toxicology awareness initiative

Goals

Our aim is to provide safety and wellbeing through advocacies for the prevention and control of toxicities from biological and non-boilogical sources.

Understanding Toxins

Toxins are harmful substances that can be found in everyday environments, from industrial chemicals to household products. Our mission is to provide clear, science-backed information about these substances.

We raise public awareness about poisoning, through webinars, workshops, research, outreaches and other innovative strategies.

The Harm They Cause

Exposure to toxins can lead to severe health issues including respiratory problems, neurological disorders, and long-term organ damage. Awareness is the first step toward prevention.

Preventing Poisoning

We provide educational programmes, workshops and community outreach initiatives to aid in the prevention of exposure to harmful substances. We aim to reduce exposure risks and provide information that will help individuals recover from toxin-related health challenges.
